# laneway redirect: privacy

The laneway redirect browser extension collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.

- It reads the address of Jira pages you open, in the browser, to pick the laneway web page to open instead.
- It sets laneway's `lw_site` cookie on your own laneway web address, to switch laneway to that Jira site.
- Its settings (laneway's address, your Jira hosts, on or off) live in the browser's extension storage. If you sync your browser, the browser syncs them; the extension's author never sees them.

No analytics, no remote code, no third parties.

Questions: [github.com/cornedor/laneway/issues](https://github.com/cornedor/laneway/issues).
